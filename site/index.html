Fire-Weather Observatory

An interactive spatial audio-visual sequencer built on NASA FIRMS fire detections. Fires appear across the map as a living instrument — watch a fire season unfold while the data itself drives the sound.

Space Jukebox

The 3D Fire Flight

A spaceship tours a 3D Earth while NASA FIRMS wildfire data becomes sound — the tone climbs over fire regions and major hotspots ping as you pass them. Narration on by default, designed audio-first for blind and low-vision listeners. Eyes optional.

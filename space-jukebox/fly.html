🚀 Ready to fly?

An auto-piloted tour of six fire regions — California, the Boreal North, the Amazon, the Mediterranean, the Sahel, and the Australian Outback — built from 330 NASA FIRMS hotspots. Sound on: the fires sing as you pass. Narration is on by default, so the whole flight works with your eyes closed. Prefer to fly yourself? Switch to Manual pilot (the Pilot button or the G key) — arrow keys steer, and an Xbox controller works too: left stick steers, triggers set speed, Start switches pilot mode.
